## What It Is + Verdict

SEO Audit is Corey Haines' marketing analytics skill that analyzes website SEO across technical, on-page, and content dimensions. It produces structured audit reports with prioritized, actionable recommendations.

## The One Thing That Matters

The skill generates prioritized recommendations, not a flat list of issues. It identifies quick wins that produce immediate ranking improvements versus strategic changes that take months. This triage is what makes the output actionable — you know what to fix today versus what to plan for next quarter.

## What's Actually Different

SEO tools like Semrush or Ahrefs provide data but require expertise to interpret. This skill runs inside Claude Code — point it at a URL or sitemap, and it produces an analysis that includes technical issues (meta tags, structured data, Core Web Vitals), on-page optimization (keyword targeting, content gaps, internal linking), and competitive positioning. It can also generate an implementation plan with specific code changes for technical fixes.

## Who It's For

Content marketers, SEO specialists, and site owners who want a quick SEO assessment without leaving their development workflow. Most valuable for small to medium sites that cannot justify enterprise SEO tool budgets. Less useful for sites already monitored by dedicated SEO platforms.

## Bottom Line

SEO Audit is a practical SEO consultant that fits in your terminal — fast, structured, and actionable.
