ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI assistant, capable of handling everything from casual conversation to complex analysis.

## Key Features

- Natural conversation
- File upload & analysis
- DALL·E image generation
- Custom GPTs

## Usage

Access ChatGPT at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) or through the mobile app.

## Configuration

Custom GPTs allow you to configure:

```yaml
# Custom GPT configuration
instructions: "You are a writing assistant..."
knowledge_files:
  - style-guide.pdf
capabilities:
  - web_search
  - dalle
  - code_interpreter
```
