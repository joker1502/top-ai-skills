Welcome to **Top AI Skills** — your curated directory for discovering the best AI skills, AI agents, and AI tools. We help developers, creators, and businesses find the right AI resources for their needs.

## Our Mission

The AI landscape evolves daily. New tools, agents, and skills emerge constantly. Our mission is to cut through the noise and curate only the most useful, well-documented, and actively maintained AI resources — so you can spend less time searching and more time building.

## What Makes Us Different

- **Curated, Not Aggregated** — Every tool is reviewed for quality, documentation, and active development
- **GitHub-First** — We prioritize tools with open-source repositories, real documentation, and active communities
- **Tutorial-Focused** — Each skill comes with practical tutorials, not just listings
- **Regularly Updated** — New skills and tutorials added weekly

## For Developers, By Curators

We understand the frustration of finding an "AI tool" that turns out to be a landing page with no substance. That's why every skill on Top AI Skills has:

- A verifiable GitHub repository with real code
- Official documentation you can actually use
- Real-world use cases with step-by-step tutorials
